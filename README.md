# Headwind

A Uniswap v4 hook for curated liquidity where the curator can never surprise you.

A curator manages the pool's price range on behalf of liquidity providers, but every
range change must be announced on-chain and only takes effect after a waiting period.
During that window, any LP who disagrees can withdraw their share before the change happens.

Built solo for the Uniswap Hook Incubator (UHI11) Hookathon.
Theme: Active Management & Curated Liquidity.
